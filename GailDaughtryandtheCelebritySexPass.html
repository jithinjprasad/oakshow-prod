    Summary: Gail Daughtry and the Celebrity Sex Pass is a 2026 American screwball comedy film directed by David Wain and written by Wain and Ken Marino. Starring Zoey Deutch alongside Jon Hamm and John Slattery, the film follows small-town Kansas hairdresser Gail Daughtry, who embarks on a madcap journey to Los Angeles with her best friend Otto to cash in on her agreed 'celebrity sex pass' with Jon Hamm after discovering her fiancé slept with Jennifer Aniston. Source:Wikipedia

    Plot

    Gail Daughtry, a vibrant hairdresser living in small-town Willowbrook, Kansas, and her fiancé Tom discuss their 'celebrity sex pass' agreement—a pre-nuptial pact allowing each one guilt-free encounter with a celebrity. Gail selects Mad Men star Jon Hamm, while Tom initially selects Tilda Swinton before switching to Jennifer Aniston after attending a book signing. When Gail catches Tom actually sleeping with Aniston, a heartbroken Gail heads to Los Angeles with her outspoken salon colleague Otto under the guise of attending a hairdressing convention. Once in Hollywood, Gail accidentally swaps luggage with Ludovica, an eccentric international criminal mastermind. Convinced by a psychic that fulfilling her celebrity pass is the only way to heal her fractured love life, Gail attempts to infiltrate high-profile parties to track down Jon Hamm while dodging bumbling assassins, finding unexpected self-discovery along the way.
