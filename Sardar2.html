    Summary: Sardar 2 (transl. Chief 2) is a 2026 Indian Tamil-language spy action thriller film directed by P. S. Mithran and produced by Prince Pictures and IVY Entertainment. Serving as both a prequel and sequel to Sardar (2022), the film stars Karthi in dual roles alongside S. J. Suryah, Malavika Mohanan, Ashika Ranganath, and Rajisha Vijayan. When an ancient, devastating biological weapon codenamed Doku threatens hundreds of millions of lives, the exiled veteran spy Chandra Bose (Sardar) must join forces with his estranged RAW operative son, Vijay Prakash, to dismantle a ruthless international mercenary network. Source:Wikipedia

    Plot

    Following the harrowing fallout of previous covert missions, veteran RAW operative Chandra Bose "Sardar" continues living under the shadow of a traitor's label in public eyes despite having sacrificed everything for his homeland. Meanwhile, his son, Inspector Vijay Prakash, has earned induction into RAW but finds himself relegated to a dormant squad handling seemingly trivial cases. The fragile status quo detonates when an apocalyptic bio-chemical weapon from Sardar's past known as Doku—capable of decimating millions within seconds—is traced by an international syndicate led by the calculating mercenary warlord Black Dagger (S. J. Suryah). With three secret encryption keys distributed among old intelligence operatives now under targeted assassination, Sardar is forced out of deep hiding. Reunited with Vijay, the father-and-son operatives race across treacherous global corridors to track down the clues, confront deep betrayal inside their ranks, and prevent a catastrophic bio-warfare outbreak before the final timer runs out.
