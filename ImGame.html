    Summary: I'm Game is a high-octane 2026 Indian Malayalam-language fantasy action thriller directed by Nahas Hidhayath (RDX) and produced by Dulquer Salmaan and Jom Varghese under Wayfarer Films. Marking Dulquer Salmaan's landmark 40th film as a lead actor (DQ40), the movie follows Dan John, a fearless high-rolling gambler and businessman whose life plunges into bizarre and mystical chaos after undergoing an unconventional hand transplant, colliding his destiny with sports, crime syndicates, and supernatural vengeance. Source:Wikipedia

    Plot

    A fearless gambler who trusts luck more than life gets caught in a series of crazy and unexpected events that turn his world upside down. Dan John, an adventurous high-stakes player, undergoes a mysterious hand transplant following a severe mishap. Soon, his newly acquired hand begins exhibiting a will, muscle memory, and ruthless instincts of its own, dragging him into the high-stakes underworld of match-fixing kingpin David Abraham and cricketer Vicky Das. What follows is a thrilling ride filled with high-voltage stunts, mind games, surprises, romance, and an adrenaline-pumping supernatural showdown. Source:IMDb
