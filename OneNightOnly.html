    Summary: One Night Only is a 2026 American romantic comedy film directed and produced by Will Gluck and written by Travis Braun. Starring Monica Barbaro and Callum Turner alongside Maya Hawke, Molly Ringwald, and Julia Fox, the film is set in an alternate near-future United States where premarital intimacy has been outlawed by federal decree except for a single 12-hour annual amnesty window. When their carefully planned evenings fall apart, two strangers cross paths amid the madness of New York City and embark on an unpredictable nocturnal odyssey, discovering that they might be seeking something far more meaningful than a temporary hookup.     Plot

    In an alternate future United States, a federal mandate known as the Chastity Accord has outlawed all premarital relations for the past three years—granting only a single 12-hour annual amnesty window known colloquially as "One Night Only." Every citizen's compliance is monitored through biometric wristbands that illuminate green during the designated window, unleashing millions into the city streets in a mad dash for consequence-free hookups. Owen (Callum Turner), a down-to-earth Brooklyn pizza shop owner, plans to spend the amnesty window proposing to his long-time girlfriend, only to be crushed when he discovers she intends to spend the night exploring other options. Meanwhile, Allie (Monica Barbaro), a hopeless romantic exasperated by modern transactional dating, vows to stay indoors and ignore the bedlam. After an accidental mishap forces Allie out into Manhattan, her path collides with Owen's in the middle of a chaotic traffic snarl. Recognizing each other's disillusionment with the citywide frenzy, the two decide to spend the night together tackling a spontaneous list of citywide misadventures—from crashing high-society rooftop galas to dodging draconian curfew patrols. As the 12-hour clock steadily ticks away toward sunrise, Allie and Owen realize that what started as an escapist friendship has blossomed into a rare, authentic romantic bond that neither wants to leave behind when the green lights go out.
