Episode Synopsis & Plot

In the past, John's father fails a trial to become a Green Lantern. Guardians of the Universe leader Lianna offers recompense by giving John a future opportunity. After serving as a Marine, John is recruited by the Guardians and chosen under Hal's mentorship. Hal welcomes John into the Corps but makes it clear that he will remain in command.
