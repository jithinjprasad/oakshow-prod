    Plot

    Sanjay Vishwanath (Suriya), a successful 40-year-old industrialist and Olympic gold-medal-winning shooter, lives within a self-built emotional cocoon due to past childhood traumas. To fulfill the wishes of his mother Nirmala Devi (Radhika Sarathkumar), Sanjay opts to have a child through surrogacy in the United States. However, when the newborn baby is diagnosed with a life-threatening illness needing an immediate bone marrow transplant, Sanjay is forced to reach out to the surrogate mother, Madanakameshwari 'Maddy' (Mamitha Baiju), a spirited 22-year-old medical student. What begins as an urgent transactional arrangement deepens as Maddy enters the Vishwanath household, challenging Sanjay's guarded life and fostering an unconventional romance that must confront past regrets, societal expectations, and healing. Source:IMDb
