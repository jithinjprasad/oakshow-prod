    Summary: G.D.N is a 2026 Indian Tamil-language biographical drama written and directed by Krishnakumar Ramakumar, starring R. Madhavan in the titular role of legendary inventor and industrialist G.D. Naidu, alongside Priyamani, Dushara Vijayan, Sathyaraj, and Jayaram. Produced by Tricolour Films and Varghese Moolan Pictures with music by Govind Vasantha, the film chronicles the extraordinary life and engineering triumphs of the man hailed as the 'Edison of India.' Source: Wikipedia

    Plot

    Set across pre- and post-independent India, the film traces the inspiring true journey of Gopalswamy Doraiswamy Naidu from his humble beginnings as an inquisitive village boy in Coimbatore to becoming one of India's most prolific mechanical inventors and visionary industrialists. Driven by an insatiable curiosity for machinery, Naidu designs India's first indigenous electric motor, innovative transportation fleets, and precision optical instruments, repeatedly defying colonial restrictions and bureaucratic roadblocks. As corporate rivals and skepticism challenge his unconventional genius, Naidu remains committed to technological self-reliance, uplifting workers and revolutionizing public transit. Supported by his wives Chellammal (Priyamani) and Ranganayaki (Dushara Vijayan), his relentless pursuit of scientific progress earns him global acclaim and the enduring moniker of the 'Edison of India.' Source: IMDb
