Description & Premise

Green Lanterns are interstellar peacekeepers who wear rings that give them extraordinary powers. The series follows experienced Lantern Hal Jordan and new recruit John Stewart as they investigate a murder in Rushville, Nebraska, which Hal believes is an extraterrestrial incident and leads them to darker mysteries and reckonings.

Main Cast with Roles
  • Kyle Chandler as Hal Jordan / Green Lantern: A USAF veteran, former test pilot and legendary member of the Green Lantern Corps who is approaching retirement and mentors new recruit John Stewart.
  • Aaron Pierre as John Stewart / Green Lantern: A new Green Lantern recruit whom Hal is training to replace himself. John is the first Green Lantern in Corps history recruited directly by the Guardians of the Universe.
  • Kelly Macdonald as Sheriff Kerry Kane: A sheriff deeply devoted to her family and close-knit town of Rushville, Nebraska.
  • Garret Dillahunt as William Macon: Kerry's father-in-law, a modern cowboy who hides his self-righteous, conspiracy-minded personality behind a charming façade.
  • Jason Ritter as Billy Macon: Kerry's lawyer husband and William's son.
  • Poorna Jagannathan as Zoe: An associate connected to the investigation.
  • J. Alphonse Nicholson as John Stewart Sr.: John's father in military flashbacks.
