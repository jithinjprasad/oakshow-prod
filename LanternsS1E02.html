Episode Synopsis & Plot

With his ring nearly depleted, Hal sends John back to Coast City to retrieve his lantern while he continues investigating the shooting. Hal uncovers alien-detecting technology and attempts to question William Macon, but is wounded by a sniper. After retrieving the power battery, John is abducted by aliens searching for an artificial Manhunter that decimated their civilization, giving John 48 hours to find it. Hal journeys off-world to interrogate Thaal Sinestro about the Manhunters.
