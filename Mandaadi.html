    Summary: Mandaadi (transl. Sea Wanderer) is a 2026 Indian Tamil-language sports action drama written and directed by Mathimaran Pugazhendhi and produced by Elred Kumar under RS Infotainment. The film stars Soori in the titular role alongside Suhas (in his Tamil debut), Mahima Nambiar, and Sathyaraj. Set against the high-stakes, physically punishing world of traditional coastal sailboat racing in Ramanathapuram, it explores rivalry, redemption, and community honor on turbulent waters. Source:Wikipedia

    Plot

    Muthukaali, a legendary traditional sailboat racer belonging to the "Mugavai Maindhans" crew in coastal Ramanathapuram, had exiled himself to work as a lifeguard on the shores of Chennai following a devastating tragedy that tore his life and community apart. In Muthukaali's absence, the ruthless and fiercely ambitious Sattapuli—once Muthukaali's trusted companion turned bitter adversary—takes command of the local seas with his formidable "Tsunami Riders" team, establishing complete supremacy and fanning fierce hostilities across neighboring fishing settlements. When an emotional plea from his beleaguered home community reaches him, Muthukaali makes his fateful return to the coastline. Confronted by fractured loyalties and a raw, inexperienced crew of local fishermen, Muthukaali must rebuild the underdog squad from scratch, overcome deep psychological trauma, and brave raging ocean squalls to compete in the grand, high-stakes festival boat regatta that will decide the fate and pride of his people.
