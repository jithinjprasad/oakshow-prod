    Plot

    Digger Rockwell (Tom Cruise), a larger-than-life energy mogul whose corporate reach spans continents, orchestrates an ambitious ultra-deep drilling initiative into the Greenland ice caps. When the colossal drill ruptures a subterranean fault, catastrophic tremors ripple across the Northern Hemisphere, triggering climate disruptions and intense political fallout in Washington. Clashing with the dictatorial President Compson (John Goodman) and his skeptical chief scientific advisor Ganesh (Riz Ahmed), Digger refuses to step down. Instead, flanked by his calculating sister Emma (Sandra Hüller) and rig engineer Kenny (Jesse Plemons), Rockwell wages a high-stakes, media-fueled spectacle across the globe to engineer an outrageous techno-miracle, betting his fortune and human survival on one final gamble.
