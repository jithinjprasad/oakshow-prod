    Plot

    Gotham City is plunged into total pandemonium when the brilliant and super-steroid enhanced strategist Bane launches a precision assault on Arkham Asylum, setting free the most dangerous criminals—including Joker, Scarecrow, Two-Face, and the Mad Hatter. Realizing he cannot defeat Batman at peak performance, Bane meticulously bleeds the hero's strength by forcing him into non-stop battles throughout the city. Suffering from severe exhaustion and refusing rest, Batman struggles to maintain control with help from Alfred and the newly recruited Jean-Paul Valley (Azrael). Having deduced Batman's identity through keen observation, Bane ambushes Bruce Wayne inside Wayne Manor and corners him in the Batcave. In an agonizing final confrontation, Bane physically overwhelms the exhausted Dark Knight and snaps his back over his knee, cementing the catastrophic fall of Gotham's protector. Source:IMDB
