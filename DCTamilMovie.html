    Summary: DC (Devadas & Chandra) is a stylish, bloody, and neo-noir gangster reimagining of the tragic classic Devdas. Featuring director Lokesh Kanagaraj in a gripping acting debut alongside Wamiqa Gabbi, the film follows a broken anti-hero descending into the dark criminal underworld fueled by heartbreak, retribution, and Anirudh's thumping score. Source:Wikipedia

    Plot

    Set against the murky, rain-soaked alleys of the coastal crime syndicate, Devadas is an uncompromising gangster whose descent into moral ruin is accelerated by his fractured romance with Chandra. When ruthless cartel kingpins orchestrate Chandra's betrayal, Devadas embarks on a merciless, gun-blazing revenge spree. As rival syndicates, corrupt law enforcers, and old friends close in, Devadas must confront his past, battling demons within and killers without in an explosive climax. Source:IMDB
