    Plot

    Ramayana: Part 1 is an upcoming Indian epic mythological drama directed by Nitesh Tiwari, serving as the monumental first chapter of a planned two-part cinematic adaptation of the ancient Sanskrit epic Ramayana by Sage Valmiki. The narrative chronicles the noble upbringing of Prince Rama in Ayodhya, his tutelage under Maharishi Vishvamitra, his divine triumph in stringing Lord Shiva's bow to win the hand of Princess Sita of Mithila, and the fateful court intrigues leading to his fourteen-year exile. Accompanied by his devoted wife Sita and brother Lakshmana, Rama journeys through the Dandakaranya forests, encountering sages and vanquishing demons, setting the stage for the epic confrontation with the formidable demon king of Lanka, Ravana. Source:IMDb / Wikipedia
